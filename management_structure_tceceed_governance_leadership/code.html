Accountability Protocols

Operational Charter & Regulatory Benchmarks

TCECEED operates under strict fiduciary mandates formulated in synergy with the Federal Ministry of Education, TETFund Intervention Guidelines, and Federal Polytechnic Nasarawa Academic Board statutes.

gavel Federal Statutory Adherence

Every procurement over threshold values passes through the Project Implementation Board under federal public procurement guidelines.

published_with_changes Bi-Annual Technical Evaluation

Independent evaluation teams deployed by TETFund assess research milestones, student enrollment ratios, and hardware production efficacy.

copyright Intellectual Property Protection

Transparent revenue-sharing framework distributing patent royalties equitably between student inventors, academic supervisors, and the Centre.
