## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, authoritative, and forward-looking academic identity for the TETFund Centre of Excellence for Clean Energy and Entrepreneurship Development (TCECEED) at Federal Polytechnic Nasarawa. 

The aesthetic reflects a balance between academic rigor and clean-tech innovation. The visual language moves away from dense, bureaucratic government portals toward a crisp, modern institutional interface rooted in corporate-academic clarity:
- **Tone & Mood:** Credible, pioneering, precise, and sustainability-driven.
- **Visual Movement:** Corporate / Modern mixed with precision data presentation. Structured grids, subtle clean-energy micro-accents, crisp outlines, and high readability ensure research papers, innovation grants, technological patents, and academic milestones take center stage.
- **Target Audience:** Academic researchers, tertiary students, renewable energy industry partners, international grant bodies, and federal policymakers.

## Colors

The color system directly honors the tripartite identity of TCECEED, TETFund, and Federal Polytechnic Nasarawa:

- **Primary (`#1B5E20` - Institutional Deep Green):** Grounded institutional foundation used for primary navigation, academic headers, key CTA buttons, and authoritative structural frames.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Clean Energy Emerald):** Represents solar, wind, and biomass innovation. Used for status indicators, active tabs, progress bars, sustainability metric badges, and secondary action highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D32F2F` - Solar Crimson Accent):** Inherited from the energetic spark and the polytechnic cogwheel crest. Reserved for critical notices, alert tags, urgent grant deadlines, and high-impact calls-to-action.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Slate Anchor):** Provides deep, high-contrast readability for body copy, paired with neutral surface layers:
  - Surface base: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White)
  - Surface muted: `#F8FAFC` (Cool Off-white)
  - Surface border: `#E2E8F0` (Architectural hairline border)

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and structural hierarchy with **Inter** for sustained technical and academic reading.

- **Headlines & Metric Counters:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Its geometric precision and clear apertures give the center's research initiatives an approachable yet state-of-the-art demeanor.
- **Body & Documentation:** Inter provides exceptional legibility across dense academic papers, curriculum modules, equipment specifications, and data tables.
- **Stat Counters & Institutional Badges:** Display bold figures with tighter tracking to accentuate scientific output, patents filed, and megawatt capacities generated.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid layout on desktop scales to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Grid Disciplines:** 
  - Desktop (≥1200px): Max width of 1280px, 24px (1.5rem) gutters, 48px (3rem) outer margins.
  - Tablet (768px – 1199px): 16px (1rem) gutters, 32px (2rem) margins.
  - Mobile (<768px): 16px gutters, 20px (1.25rem) margins.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major research sections and portal modules rely on `space-2xl` (64px) vertical section paddings, creating structured academic pacing without cluttered visual density.
- **Reflow Rules:** Complex multi-column statistics and research card grids collapse to 2 columns on tablet and single-column swipeable decks on mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of **tonal surface layering** and **low-contrast outlines**, backed by restrained ambient shadows. This keeps the portal scholarly rather than overly commercial:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure neutral background (`#F8FAFC` or `#FFFFFF`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Panel Surface):** Flat `#FFFFFF` bound by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards / Floating Sliders):** Raised on hover or active state. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.08)`, introducing a green-tinted depth.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Global Mega Menus):** High elevation layer. Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (Moderate Rounded):
- Standard components (buttons, input fields, badges): `0.5rem` (8px).
- Content containers, research project cards, hero banners: `1rem` (16px).
- Modal dialogs and stat ticker pods: `1.5rem` (24px).
- Institutional status tags & category pills: Full pill (`9999px`) to maintain contrast against rectangular card frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Solid `#1B5E20` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius, horizontal padding `1.25rem`, vertical padding `0.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#164D1B` with subtle scale enhancement (`1.01`).
- **Clean Tech Secondary Button:** Solid `#10B981` fill with `#FFFFFF` text for entrepreneurship and innovation actions.
- **Tertiary / Action Alert:** Outlined or solid `#D32F2F` strictly for urgent funding deadlines or regulatory alerts.
- **Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), `#1E293B` text; on hover, `#F1F5F9` background and `#1B5E20` text.

### Research & Project Cards
- Rendered on Level 1 white cards with a 1px border.
- Features an energy-sector tag (Solar, Biomass, Wind, Entrepreneurship) in the top-left using pill badges.
- Includes publication date, lead investigator, and an emerald-accented progress bar for ongoing research initiatives.

### Institutional Badges & Tickers
- **Metric Badges:** Pill-shaped, subtle green background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), emerald text (`#047857`), and a micro green dot indicator.
- **Stat Ticker Pods:** Compact cards containing `stat-counter` typography in `#1B5E20`, accompanied by an icon and an uppercase `label-sm` description in slate grey.

### Sliders & Carousel Components
- Full-bleed or inset research showcase sliders.
- Clean dot indicators styled as 8px circles; active slide indicator stretches to a 24px pill in `#10B981`.
- Navigation arrows feature 40px circular white buttons with 1px border and a subtle Level 2 shadow.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 44px minimum touch target, `0.5rem` border radius, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), background `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus state: Border color changes to `#10B981` with an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox (`rounded-sm`) and circular radio in 18px dimensions.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`.
- Checked: `#1B5E20` fill with a sharp white checkmark or center pip.
