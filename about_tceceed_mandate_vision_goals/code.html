Established by Presidential Directive to Solve Nigeria's Decentralized Energy Paradox

The TETFund Centre of Excellence for Clean Energy and Entrepreneurship Development (TCECEED) at The Federal Polytechnic Nasarawa was founded under the auspices of the Federal Ministry of Education and the Tertiary Education Trust Fund (TETFund). Established to serve as a beacon of academic rigor and technological autonomy, the Centre answers the urgent call to decentralize clean electricity generation.

Nigeria faces an acute structural energy challenge: frequent national grid collapses, urban power access lingering under 50%, and rural access dropping below 10%. TCECEED acts as the strategic catalyst bridge—translating advanced research in photovoltaic systems, organic biomass, and hybrid storage into commercial prototypes and market-ready enterprise incubations.
