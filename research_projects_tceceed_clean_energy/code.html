Stream 02 • Fabrication Yard

Mechanical Fabrication & Low-Speed Wind Turbines

Prototyping horizontal and vertical-axis wind turbines optimized for the low-to-medium wind speed corridors (3.2 m/s – 6.5 m/s) of Central Nigeria. All tubular masts, bearing housings, and furling tail fins are cast and welded inside our Federal Poly engineering workshops.

Stream 03 • Applied Electromechanical

Hybrid Clean Energy Mobile Inverter Carts

Modular wheel-mounted energy stations coupling automotive alternators, dual-frequency pulse transformers, and deep-cycle lithium-ferro phosphate packs. Built to replace smoky petrol generators for small-scale market vendors, welding kiosks, and mobile medical screening booths.

Patent Pending (NG/PT/2024/091) Schematic chevron_right
Agro-Industrial Pilot Stream 04

Anaerobic Biogas Digestion & Bio-Fertilizer Refinery

Conversion of livestock slurry, municipal organic sludge, and cassava peels from Nasarawa agricultural markets into scrubbing-grade methane gas (CH4 > 65%). Digested bio-effluent is packaged as pathogen-free organic nitrogen fertilizer, closing the ecological cycle for local farming cooperatives.

Project Alpha Phase 2: Operational

Off-Grid Solar 'Power-Box' for Primary Health Centres

Custom ruggedized DC micro-power containers supplying 24/7 uninterrupted power for vaccine refrigerators, obstetrics lighting, and ultrasound diagnostic scanners in 6 rural clinics across Nasarawa Local Government.

Deployment Progress 85% (5 of 6 Clinics)

Beneficiary Count: 18,400+ Rural Patients
Grant Sponsor: TETFund NRF 2023/2024
Ref: TCE-PRJ-24-001
Project Beta Phase 3: Validation

Cassava Wastewater & Agro-Waste Bio-Digesters

Tackling cyanide-rich toxicity from regional garri processing mills by co-digesting cassava peel effluent with bovine manure in continuous high-yield anaerobic batch digesters to furnish piped clean cooking gas for student halls.

Validation Testing 92% Chemical Safety
Lead Investigator: Dr. (Mrs.) H. U. Garba
Cyanide Degradation: 99.1% Total Reduction
Grant Sponsor: Institutional Seed Grant
Ref: TCE-PRJ-24-004
Project Gamma Phase 1: Prototyping

Indigenous Pure Sine-Wave Inverter & Charge Controller

Empowering Federal Poly Nasarawa electrical apprentice artisans to print single-layer PCBs, wind high-temperature copper toroidal transformers, and calibrate micro-controllers locally, ending foreign component dependency.

Unit Cost Reduction: -48% vs Imported Kits
