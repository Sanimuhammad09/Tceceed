Internship Programme Framework

Built under strict TETFund Centre of Excellence governance standards to bridge the divide between theoretical diplomas and workplace execution.

Maximum Period

The internship spans a continuous maximum duration of 6 Months under full-time professional oversight from TCECEED engineers and resident fellows.

school

Eligibility Criteria

Open strictly to recent graduates of ND (National Diploma), HND (Higher National Diploma), and B.Sc / B.Eng in technical & scientific disciplines.

payments

Terms & Allowances

Interns receive targeted practical training modules, active supervisor mentorship, a monthly financial stipend, and complete a high-impact capstone project.

Cohorts are publicized periodically through official channels. Selection is strictly merit-based, governed by aptitude reviews and practical potential.
