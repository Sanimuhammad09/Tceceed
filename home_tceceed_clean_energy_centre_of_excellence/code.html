Tackling Nigeria’s Energy Deficit Through Groundbreaking Applied Science

The TETFund Centre of Excellence for Clean Energy and Entrepreneurship Development (TCECEED) at The Federal Polytechnic Nasarawa was established under the approval of the Presidency and the Federal Ministry of Education to confront Nigeria's persistent power sector crises.

With national grid connectivity hovering below 50% in major cities and plummeting under 10% across rural agrarian communities, TCECEED translates clean energy research into affordable, commercially viable hardware. We engineer practical off-grid solar kits, agricultural biogas digesters, and clean heating technologies while fostering high-growth clean-tech enterprises.
