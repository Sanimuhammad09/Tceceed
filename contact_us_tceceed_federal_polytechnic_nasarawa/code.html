By Road From Abuja (FCT)

Approx. 95 km (1.5 - 2 hours drive) via the Abuja-Keffi Expressway branching south at Keffi toward Nasarawa town. The polytechnic campus is situated along the main arterial corridor.

flight_land

Nearest Aviation Hub

Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport, Abuja (ABV). Chartered institutional transit can be arranged via the TCECEED secretariat for approved delegations and keynote fellows.
